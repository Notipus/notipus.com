Something not working, or not sure how to set it up? Get in touch and a human will help.

## Contact us

**Email: [support@notipus.com](mailto:support@notipus.com)**

That is the fastest way to reach us, and it is the right address for everything: setup problems,
notifications that did not arrive, billing and invoices, cancelling, feature requests, security
reports, and privacy or data requests.

No account, signup, or support portal is needed to contact us. Just send an email.

**We reply to every request within two business days**, and usually much sooner. We are based in the
United Kingdom, so our working hours are Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm UK time.

To help us fix things on the first reply, it is useful (but never required) to include:

- Your workspace name
- Which event source is involved: Stripe, Shopify, or Maxio (Chargify)
- Where notifications should be arriving: Slack, Telegram, or Microsoft Teams
- Roughly when the problem happened, and what you expected to see instead

## Other ways to reach us

These are optional extras. Both need a GitHub account, so email remains the primary channel if you
would rather not sign up for anything.

- [Report a bug or request a feature](https://github.com/Notipus/Notipus/issues) on GitHub
- [Browse the source](https://github.com/Notipus/Notipus) — Notipus is open source, and self-hosting
  notes are in the README

## Security and privacy

To report a security vulnerability, email [support@notipus.com](mailto:support@notipus.com) and we
will acknowledge it within two business days. Please do not open a public GitHub issue for security
problems.

For data access, correction, or deletion requests, email the same address. Our [privacy
policy]({{< ref "privacy.md" >}}) explains what we store, how long we keep it, and the rights you
have over it.
