how-it-works

What the Slack app actually does

When you connect Slack, Notipus asks Slack for permission to post into a single channel that you select during installation. Slack creates an incoming webhook scoped to just that channel, and Notipus uses it to deliver messages as they happen.

Notipus posts to Slack. It does not read conversations, it does not join other channels, and it does not message your teammates directly. There are no slash commands and no interactive buttons that send data back to Notipus: the buttons on each message are plain links that open your payment provider's dashboard or an email draft.

You can change the destination channel at any time from the Notipus integrations screen, and removing the app from your Slack workspace stops delivery immediately.

Permissions we request, and why

Scope Why Notipus needs it
incoming-webhook Creates the webhook for the one channel you choose at install time. This is how every notification is delivered.
chat:write Posts the notification messages themselves.
channels:read Lists your public channels so you can pick a destination from a dropdown instead of typing a channel ID.

That is the complete list. Notipus requests no scopes for reading messages, files, or user profiles.

installation

Installing Notipus in Slack

Notipus is a multi-tenant service, so the Slack connection attaches to a Notipus workspace. That means you create an account first, then connect Slack from inside the product. It takes a couple of minutes.

  1. Create a free Notipus account

    Go to app.notipus.com and sign up with a passkey or with your Slack account. A free workspace is created for you automatically, with no card required.

  2. Open Integrations and choose Slack

    On the Integrations screen, Slack sits under Notification Channels on the right. Press Connect to start the Slack authorisation flow. This is where the Add to Slack button lives once you are signed in.

  3. Approve the permissions and pick a channel

    Slack shows you the three scopes listed above and asks which channel Notipus may post to. Choose the channel your team watches for revenue, then approve. You must be a Slack workspace admin, or have permission to install apps, to complete this step.

  4. Connect a payment source

    Back in Notipus, connect Stripe, Shopify, or Maxio (Chargify) from the Event Sources column. Each one is a short webhook setup, and the guides walk through it step by step. Your first alert lands in Slack as soon as the next payment event fires.

Signed out? This takes you to the Notipus sign-in page first, then straight into the Slack authorisation screen.

privacy

What Notipus stores, and where to reach us

Notipus receives payment events from the billing tools you connect, enriches them with publicly available company information, and delivers the result to your Slack channel. Slack credentials are stored encrypted and used only to post to the channel you selected. We do not sell data, and we do not use your data to train models.

Our privacy policy sets out exactly what we collect, how long we keep it, which subprocessors are involved, and how to request access or deletion. It is written against the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Need help or have a question before installing? Our support page has the details, or email support@notipus.com and we reply within two business days.
