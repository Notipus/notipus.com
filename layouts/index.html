what-you-get

More than a webhook relay

Zapier tells you a payment happened. Notipus tells you who it was, what they're worth, and whether they're about to churn.

Every event, in seconds

New subscriptions, upgrades, downgrades, failed payments, trial conversions, cancellations, refunds — from webhook to Slack in seconds. The failed renewal you catch today is a customer you can still save.

Customer context built in

Every alert includes the company's background and size, the contact's name and role, lifetime value, and email badges for education, government, free, and disposable domains — enriched automatically. No more TPS reports.

Insights, not noise

VIP and at-risk flags, lifetime-value milestones, and trial-conversion alerts — with duplicate events consolidated into a single message instead of five pings.
